# 🎬 Kwaresma (2019)

> *"Some secrets should never be unearthed."*

## Overview

**Kwaresma** is a Filipino horror film directed by `Erik Matti`. It combines elements of supernatural horror with themes of family, trauma, and faith.

## 🧠 Key Details

- **Genre:** Horror, Supernatural  
- **Director:** `Erik Matti`  
- **Starring:**  
  - Sharon Cuneta  
  - John Arcilla  
  - Kent Gonzales

## 🔥 Why I Like Kwaresma

1. `Atmospheric Horror` – The film builds dread through mood rather than jump scares.  
2. `Strong Performances` – Sharon Cuneta delivers a haunting, emotional performance.  
3. `Cultural Depth` – It explores Filipino beliefs about spirits, religion, and guilt.

## 🎭 Memorable Quote

> “May mga bagay na hindi mo dapat pinapakialaman.”  
> (*There are things you shouldn’t mess with.*)
